My Journey

My academic journey began in the UAE, where I completed my schooling before moving to Canada as an international student. A scholarship allowed me to pursue a Bachelor of Business Administration at Trent University, an experience that shaped both my career path and worldview.

Student life had its challenges, but I learned the power of asking questions and seeking help. Office hours became a space where supportive professors helped me grow in confidence and understanding.

Trent’s campus, surrounded by nature, offered not only excellent academics but also a caring community. From student events to emotional support resources, it fostered both learning and well-being.

Deeply connected to the traditional territory of the Mississauga Anishinaabeg, Trent’s commitment to reconciliation gave me a meaningful understanding of Indigenous history and culture.

My time there taught me resilience, adaptability, and the value of connection—lessons I carry forward into every new opportunity.
